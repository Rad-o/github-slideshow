Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need Git installed to start?

No. The slideshow runs entirely in your browser and the exercises use GitHub's web interface, so you can start learning without installing anything locally.

Is this course suitable for absolute beginners?

Yes. The course starts from the very basics—what version control is and why it matters—before moving on to branching, pull requests, and automation.

How long does the course take?

Most learners complete the core slides in 60–90 minutes. The hands-on GitHub exercises add another 1–2 hours of practice depending on your pace.

Can I use this slideshow to teach my team?

Absolutely. The project is open source under the MIT licence. Fork it, customise the slides, and present it in workshops or team onboarding sessions.
